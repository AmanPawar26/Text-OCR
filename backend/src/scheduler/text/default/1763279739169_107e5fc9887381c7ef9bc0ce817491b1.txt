Title: Outputs of the Monitor and Control Project Work process

Body Text:
45. Outputs of the Monitor and Control Project Work process include all of the following except:

A. Change requests.

B. Project management plan updates.

C. Project document updates.

D. Final product, service, or result transition.

46. Actions and activities necessary to satisfy completion or exit criteria for the phase or project and to transfer the project's products, services, or results to the next phase or to production and/or operations are addressed:
